Samsung Washer Error Code 4C

Easy DIYChecked against Samsung documentationUpdated Nov 2026

What it means

Water supply error (4C, older models 4E) — the washer is not filling with water properly.

Most likely causes

  • Water taps closed or not fully open
  • Clogged mesh filters in the water inlets
  • Kinked inlet hose or low water pressure
  • Frozen hoses in winter, or a failed inlet valve

Safe checks you can do yourself

  1. Confirm both supply taps are fully open and water pressure is normal elsewhere in the home.
  2. Close the taps, disconnect the inlet hoses at the washer and clean the mesh filters.
  3. Check the hoses for kinks and make sure the drain hose is not inserted too far into the standpipe (which can siphon water out as fast as it fills).
  4. Restart the cycle. Persistent 4C with good pressure means the inlet valve or its wiring needs testing.

What a repair usually costs

$0 for taps/filters. Inlet valve: roughly $130–$250 fitted.

When to call a professional

Call a pro if 4C returns with verified pressure and clean filters.

✔Verified source: this guide was checked against Samsung's official documentation for this code, and cross-checked with technician references, on 26 Nov 2026.
Safety first: unplug the washer and close its water taps before opening any panel or filter. Codes can vary slightly between models — your user manual is the final reference for your exact machine.
